# Product Updates

## Identity
- SSO is generally available.
- Automated user provisioning is planned but not yet generally available.

## Authorization
- Current product supports two roles: Admin and User.
- Granular role-based access controls are under consideration but are not committed to the roadmap.

## Auditability
- User activity is logged internally.
- Customers cannot currently export their own audit logs.
